Help & instructions

Everything you need to get moving.

⌁How to connect

  1. Pair your HC-05 or HC-06 in your device's Bluetooth settings (PIN 1234 or 0000).
  2. Open this app in Chrome or Edge over HTTPS or localhost.
  3. Click Connect Bluetooth and select the paired module's outgoing serial port.
  4. Look for the green Connected badge. The serial speed is 9600 baud.

⌖Controls guide

Use the six sliders to control the base, shoulder, elbow, wrist roll, wrist pitch, and gripper. Reset / Home returns each joint to 90°. Save poses to build a sequence, then run it with your chosen delay and optional loop.

⚡Safety & power tips

Move sliders smoothly, one at a time. Rapid movement across all six servos can cause battery voltage drops. Avoid forcing or over-tightening the gripper.
